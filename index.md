I am an assistant professor in Philosophy of Science at the Institute for Philosophy of the [University of the Republic](https://udelar.edu.uy). 

My research focuses on the foundations of cognitive science, especially theories of concepts and reasoning. I’m also interested in social epistemology, non-classical logics (notably nonmonotonic and relevant logics), philosophy of science (with emphasis on conceptual change and scientific rationality), and philosophy of language (particularly inferentialism).

I studied philosophy, logic, and cognitive science at the University of the Republic, the University of Paris 1, and LMU. I completed my PhD under the supervision of Stephan Hartmann and Max Kistler at the [Munich Center for Mathematical Philosophy](https://www.mcmp.philosophie.uni-muenchen.de/index.html) and the Institut d’histoire et de philosophie des sciences et des techniques. After that, I was a NeuroMind Fellow at the [Center for Mind and Cognition](https://philosophy-cognition.com/cmc/) at Ruhr-Universität Bochum and a postdoctoral researcher at the [Institute for Philosophy](https://www.philosophie.hhu.de/en/) at Heinrich-Heine Universität Düsseldorf.
