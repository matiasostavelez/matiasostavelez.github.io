I am an assistant professor in Philosophy of Science at the Institute for Philosophy of the [University of the Republic](https://udelar.edu.uy). 

My research focuses on the foundations of cognitive science, particularly theories of concepts and theories of reasoning. Other areas of interest include social epistemology, non-classical logics (especially nonmonotonic and relevant logics), general philosophy of science (with an emphasis on conceptual change and scientific rationality), and philosophy of language (particularly inferentialism).

I studied philosophy, logic, and cognitive science at the University of the Republic, the University of Paris 1, and LMU. I completed my PhD under the supervision of Stephan Hartmann and Max Kistler at the Munich Center for Mathematical Philosophy and the Institut d’histoire et de philosophie des sciences et des techniques. After that, I was a NeuroMind Fellow at the [Center for Mind and Cognition](https://philosophy-cognition.com/cmc/) at Ruhr-Universität Bochum and a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute for Philosophy at Heinrich Heine Universität Düsseldorf.
